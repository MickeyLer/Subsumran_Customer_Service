# Product

## Register

product

## Users
The primary users are loan and gold-saving customers of Subsumran Pico (บจก. ทรัพย์สำราญ พีโก) in Thailand, accessing the application as a LINE LIFF (LINE Front-end Framework) dashboard directly inside the LINE Messenger app. Their physical context is mobile-first, under varied ambient lighting, and often in a hurry. They need to quickly check upcoming loan payment details, copy bank accounts, upload receipts, review their active contracts, or check gold savings rates.

## Product Purpose
The application serves as a premium, highly tactile customer service portal that simplifies financial transactions (loan installment payments, gold savings management) and increases trust. Success looks like frictionless payment submission, reduced service inquiries to staff, and high confidence in transactions through clear confirmation states.

## Brand Personality
- **Trustworthy**: Inspires financial security, safety, and legitimacy.
- **Approachable**: Highly friendly and accessible to everyday Thai users across all generations and tech-literacy levels.
- **Prestigious**: Uses a refined, premium color palette (Navy, Gold, Warm Cream) that communicates high-quality service and financial excellence.

## Anti-references
- **Over-complicated Bank Apps**: Cluttered interfaces, excessively dense financial jargon, and tedious nested navigation that cause high cognitive load.
- **Shady Loan Apps**: Tacky and aggressive color schemes, flashing animations, and high-pressure marketing banners that look unprofessional or untrustworthy.
- **Dry/Bland Tables**: Unstyled, grey raw data tables and text forms that fail to deliver a premium, comforting user experience.

## Design Principles
1. **Tactile Simplicity**: Use clear bento-grid layouts, recognizable iconography, and satisfying interactive feedback to make the mobile experience feel physically responsive.
2. **Clear Financial Reassurance**: Highlight key transaction states (paid, pending, copy success) using visual hierarchy rather than dense text.
3. **Thai-centric Comfort**: Ensure all labels, instruction flows, and error messages are written in welcoming, professional, and clear Thai language.
4. **Instant Actionability**: Keep the primary tasks (making a payment, copying a bank number, starting gold savings) within a single tap of the home viewport.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- **Typography & Readability**: Clear Thai font scaling (Sarabun / Be Vietnam Pro) with high text contrast (body text ≥4.5:1 against background) for readability across older age groups.
- **Tactile Target Size**: Large, distinct touch targets (minimum 44x44px) suitable for mobile screen navigation.
- **Motion Restraint**: Gentle transitions and micro-animations with standard fallback rules when reduced motion is preferred.
